Step 02

Our AI Reads Every Clause

Three analysis pipelines run simultaneously.

Three Pipelines. Six Detection Modules.

Our AI runs six detection modules across three core analysis pipelines. See our Features page for the full breakdown.

1
Statute Cross-Reference Pipeline
Powers our Compliance Risk Detection and All-50-States Statute Database modules — comparing every clause against your state's landlord-tenant statutes and flagging language that may not be enforceable.
2
Fee Analysis Pipeline
Powers our Junk Fee Scanner — identifying every monetary charge and benchmarking it against state caps and market norms.
3
Predatory Language Pipeline
Powers our Predatory Term Detection — surfacing vague, one-sided, or unusually aggressive language that puts renters at unfair disadvantage.

Background Processes
