Frequently Asked Questions
Is LeaseGuard AI really free?
Yes — your free preview includes the Lease Health Score (A–F grade), your top flagged clauses (title, severity, and the exact lease text), plus your state’s key tenant rights (deposit limit & notice period). Upgrading to Pro ($19.99 one-time) unlocks the full report: every red flag with legal explanations, relevant statutes, recommended negotiation actions, ready-to-use templates, the complete state law guide, and a PDF copy via email.
Is my lease stored or shared?
Your lease document itself is never stored — it's processed in memory and discarded as soon as analysis completes. Your analysis results (including short lease-text snippets in each flag) are retained for up to 7 days so you can re-download your report or upgrade to Pro without re-uploading. After 7 days, they're permanently deleted (Pro reports saved to a customer account are kept until you delete them). We never sell, share, or use your lease content to train AI models. Full details on our Privacy page.
Does LeaseGuard AI replace a lawyer?
No — and we're upfront about that. LeaseGuard AI is an informational tool, not a law firm. We don't give legal advice. For legal interpretation of how a clause applies to your specific situation, or for any active dispute, please consult a licensed attorney in your state. Many states have free tenant-rights legal aid offices — we link to them in our tenant-rights guides.
Which states are supported?
All 50 U.S. states. Our database includes current landlord-tenant statutes, consumer protection laws, and relevant case law for every state.
How accurate is the AI?
Our model is trained on publicly available U.S. landlord-tenant statutes and tenant-protection guidance. Every flag links to its underlying source so you can verify the language directly. AI gets things wrong sometimes — that's why we always recommend you verify any flag yourself, and consult a licensed attorney for legal interpretation specific to your situation.
What file types do you support?
We accept standard PDF leases (up to 25 pages), scanned PDF leases (OCR-enhanced), and copy-pasted lease text. Multi-addenda packages are also supported.
I have a signing deadline — how fast is this really?
Most analyses complete in under 60 seconds after upload. Even for complex leases over 20 pages, you typically have your full report in under 3 minutes — fast enough for any deadline.
