Pricing FAQ
Everything you need to know before you pay anything.
Is this a subscription or a one-time payment?
Pro and Premium are monthly subscriptions. You can cancel at any time from your account dashboard — cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. If you only need a single lease check, we also offer a one-time Pro analysis for $19.99.
What's the $19.99 one-time option?
If you only need to check one lease and don't want a recurring plan, the $19.99 one-time analysis gives you a full Pro-level report for that lease — no subscription, no recurring charge.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay via Stripe. Payments are processed securely — we never see your card details.
Can I get a refund?
If you're unsatisfied with your analysis, contact us within 48 hours for a no-questions-asked refund. For subscriptions, you can cancel any time and retain access through your paid period.
Why does the Free plan cap at 5 flags?
The Free preview gives you the Lease Health Score and your top 5 highest-risk flags, fully explained. If your lease has 5 flags or fewer, you'll get the complete picture for free. If it has more — and most leases do — Pro or a one-time $19.99 analysis unlocks everything.
What is Deposit Shield?
Deposit Shield is an upcoming Premium feature that helps you document your unit condition at move-in, track landlord communications, and build a record that supports deposit return disputes. It's currently in development.
