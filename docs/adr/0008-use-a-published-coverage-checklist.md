# 0008. Use a published coverage checklist

Blueline reports missing protections only against a small, published lease checklist. The model does not decide anew for each lease what protections ought to exist, and a signer's personal red lines are not the sole source of expectations. This makes not-found items consistent and open to challenge, at the cost of omitting protections outside the published checklist.

The first checklist covers both money terms and rights or remedies: deposit deductions and return, early-exit costs, how rent can change, repairs, access to the home, and dispute routes. A missing item reports only that the relevant term was not located in the complete agreement; it is not, by itself, a legal conclusion or a risk flag. Covering both groups from the start makes the checklist broader and increases the number of absences a signer must interpret.
