# 0012. Require independent trust evidence before expansion

Blueline will not expand beyond lease signers merely because users say a review feels helpful or express willingness to pay. Expansion first requires independent review showing that source quotes can be verified against the lease and serious flags are reliably caught. This is the trust claim the first version exists to prove.

The choice leaves willingness to pay for a standalone AI review unresolved, even though the research identified it as a major gap. A useful-feeling result is not sufficient evidence that the analysis can be trusted.
