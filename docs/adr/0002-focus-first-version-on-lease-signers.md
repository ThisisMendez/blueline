# 0002. Focus the first version on lease signers

Blueline's first version is for renters who have a residential lease in hand and must decide soon whether to sign or renew it. Lease concerns have concrete evidence in the research, and this gives the product a specific decision to support. The first version will not serve subscription and Terms of Service readers; the direct pain evidence for that audience is weakest. Freelance and employment agreements may matter later, but they do not set first-version priorities.

This choice gives up a broad pool of casual documents and offers little to people learning about contracts in general or seeking help after a dispute has begun. The team will judge the brief against the lease signer's immediate decision, rather than treating every document type as equally important.
