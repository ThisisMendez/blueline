# 0007. Separate absences and preferences from risk flags

An expected protection missing from the complete agreement appears as a distinct “not found” item, not a risk flag. There is no sentence to cite for an absence, and the existing rule requires an exact source sentence for every risk flag. A not-found item reports what was not located; it does not claim that the lease is unlawful.

A signer's personal red lines produce preference matches in a separate result category. They do not change the general severity of risk flags: a term can be decisive for one renter without being generally severe. This costs the reader more than one list to understand, but keeps document-backed risks, missing protections, and personal preferences from being mistaken for one another.
