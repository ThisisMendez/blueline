# 0003. Rank lease flags by their consequence to the signer

The first lease-review priorities are deposit forfeiture, substantial early termination penalties, and waivers of dispute rights. These are a starting shortlist, not an exhaustive list: another clause can be flagged when its terms create a plausible, material downside for the lease signer. Common wording can still be dangerous; unusual wording alone is not a reason to warn.

Severity follows the potential effect on the signer's decision. A potentially decision-changing loss or rights waiver is high; a meaningful but bounded cost is medium; a minor effect is low or is not flagged. This requires Blueline to explain the scenario behind a serious warning and risks underplaying a bounded fee that is severe for a particular renter. It also gives up using rarity or apparent legal invalidity as shortcuts for severity.
