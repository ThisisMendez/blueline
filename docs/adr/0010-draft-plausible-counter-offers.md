# 0010. Draft plausible counter-offers

For each risk flag, Blueline drafts a proposed change aimed at reducing the signer's risk while remaining plausible for a landlord to accept. It does not automatically demand the strongest possible protection or replace a proposed edit with only a clarification question. A plausible compromise may leave some risk in the lease, so the review must not imply that accepting the draft would remove the risk entirely.

The proposed edit appears through a “See proposed edit” action after the signer opens the flag's explanation. The signer encounters the consequence before the negotiation language. This adds a step and means some signers may never discover the draft.
