# Blueline Lease legal and privacy operations

These materials are self-drafted for the free beta and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Alexis Mendez operates Blueline Lease as an individual in Rhode Island. The only published email address is `support@bluelinelease.com`. Keep the forwarding mailbox, signed agreements, account identifiers, and operational case records in restricted storage outside Git.

The public policies come from [the application's policy text](../../src/features/site/legal-content.ts). Current routes are `/terms` and `/privacy`. The initial permanent versions are `/terms/v1` and `/privacy/v1`.

| File | What it records |

| [Legal changelog](CHANGELOG.md) | Policy releases and notice procedure |
| [Data map](data-map.md) | Where data flows and what operations use it |
| [Subprocessors](subprocessors.md) | Services, processing roles, and status |

| [Vendor DPAs](vendor-dpas.md) | Published agreements and confirmation steps |

Review this folder when a vendor, data category, service location, policy, or retention practice changes. Before release, verify that the support address receives mail and that the effective date matches the planned publication. An approved app change does not establish a signed vendor agreement or legal review.
