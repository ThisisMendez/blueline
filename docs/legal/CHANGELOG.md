Keep each published version at its original versioned route and record the publication date and commit. Update the current policy route and signup version only when a new version takes effect. For changes to one policy, increment only that policy. Keep a private copy of each notice and record when and where it appeared.

For a material change, write a short summary of the change and its effect on people using the app. Give advance notice through the site or an authorized account-email process. Use the verified support address and individual or privacy-preserving delivery where appropriate, subject to provider limits. Confirm recipients and permission before sending. Never include lease text, personal details about other users, or a recipient list. Verify the current and versioned pages before and after the effective date. Ask counsel which changes need renewed agreement, including for existing accounts. Existing accounts do not have retroactive agreement records.
