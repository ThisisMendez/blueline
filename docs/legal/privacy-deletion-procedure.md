# Handling deletion requests during the beta

Draft for the operator. The proposed commitments are five business days to acknowledge a request and 30 calendar days from receipt to complete a verified request or explain an applicable exception. Confirm applicable privacy rights and verification requirements before publishing the policy.

1. Watch the support inbox for Formspree submissions with the subject `[Blue Line] Website Contact` and the topic `Privacy or my data`. Record a request ID, receipt date, scope, due dates, and status in a private log. Keep lease text and account secrets out of the log.
2. Locate the account using the email on the request. Reply to the verified account address and ask the person to confirm the request and its scope from that address. Treat the email typed into the public form as unverified. Do not ask for a password, login code, lease PDF, or government ID. If the person cannot access the address, escalate for a safe verification path.
3. For a single report, match the confirmed report to its owner before removing it in Supabase. For account deletion, verify the account again before using the authorized admin process. The database schema links the account to reviews and red lines with cascading deletes. Check the affected records and associated review documents as part of the operation.
4. Check the other places the request covers. Formspree stores submissions and Gmail receives a notification copy. On Formspree Free, the dashboard shows 30 days of history while older submissions can remain stored. Check with the vendor for older or inaccessible copies. Check provider logs, backups, and any retention exceptions before describing what can be removed. [Formspree account limits](https://help.formspree.io/articles/account-management/account-limits)
5. Reply to the verified address with the result and any limits. Keep only the record needed to show how the request was handled. Get counsel's advice on the retention period for that record.

On November 15, 2026, production Supabase showed `blueline-purge-expired-reviews` active every minute and a successful latest run. The scheduled job and the manual request process serve different purposes. Before publishing the policy, rehearse the manual process with an authorized synthetic account and a review, then verify that the expired review job also removes stored documents in a hosted test.
