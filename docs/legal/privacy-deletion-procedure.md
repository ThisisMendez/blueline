# Handling privacy requests during the beta

Alexis Mendez is responsible for monitoring support@bluelinelease.com. If the verified web form is available, also look for the subject `[Blueline Lease] Website Contact` and the topic `Privacy or my data`. Acknowledge each request within five business days. Complete verified access, correction, copy, and deletion requests within 30 calendar days of receipt or explain any applicable exception.

1. Record a request identifier, intake time, type, scope, deadline and status in the private log. Keep account secrets and lease text out of the log.
2. For an account request, find the registered email and ask the person to confirm scope from that address. A form's reply field is not proof of control. For requests without an account, use a proportionate verification method based on the specific interaction and information at issue. Never ask for passwords, one-time codes, lease PDFs or government ID as a routine verification step. Escalate if identity or authority to act on someone else's behalf is unclear.
3. Locate covered data. For access or copies, collect only material tied to the verified person, check third-party information and send it using a method appropriate to its sensitivity. For correction, verify the requested change and update the relevant store without altering historical policy-version evidence. For review deletion, confirm review ID and ownership before deleting. For account deletion, use the authorized Supabase admin process and check the account's linked reviews, document rows, and red lines.
4. Check other copies included in the request. For contact messages, check both the receipt mailbox and Formspree. Older Formspree Free submissions can remain stored after they disappear from the dashboard. Ask the vendor about copies you cannot access. Assess applicable logs, backups and legal holds before making any promise that copies are gone. [Formspree limits](https://help.formspree.io/articles/account-management/account-limits)
5. Explain what was completed, any exception or delay that applies, and available next steps. Keep minimal evidence of the response. Test the entire process with an authorized synthetic account before relying on it.
