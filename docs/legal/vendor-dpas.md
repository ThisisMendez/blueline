# Vendor data processing agreements

The operator reviews and accepts any agreement that requires binding acceptance. Save evidence of applicability, agreement revision, account/plan, date and authorized acceptance in restricted storage. A vendor link does not establish a signed agreement. Start with the existing free services.

| Service | Published terms and action |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase | Use the official DPA request/view entry. Confirm applicability to the production Free organization and obtain any agreement record available to that organization. [Supabase DPA](https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum) · [DPA guide](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/security/gdpr-compliance) |
| Vercel | The published DPA specifies Enterprise and Pro. Confirm the terms that apply to the actual plan and ask Vercel or counsel about coverage before claiming a DPA. Any upgrade requires separate owner approval. [Vercel DPA](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa) |
| OpenRouter | The standard DPA is incorporated through its Terms. Record the accepted revision and ask how the account and pinned Fireworks route handle review payloads. Section 2.6 excludes sensitive data absent a separate agreement. Check actual logs and settings, scope of restricted data and any necessary product controls. [OpenRouter DPA](https://openrouter.ai/data-processing-agreement) · [Terms](https://openrouter.ai/terms) |
| Fireworks AI | The deployment reaches Fireworks through OpenRouter. Verify applicable model-provider terms with OpenRouter. Document any retention or region evidence before relying on it. [Fireworks Trust Center](https://trust.fireworks.ai/) |
| Resend | If enabled, verify the account's terms acceptance and record the DPA revision. Resend's DPA ties its effective date to acceptance of the incorporated Terms of Service. [Resend DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) |
| Cloudflare | If processing is enabled, review Cloudflare's public DPA and confirm the operator's accepted agreement or explicit acceptance. Save applicable plan and service scope. [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) |
| Formspree | If enabled, ask Formspree for the agreement and confirm whether it applies to this Free account and whether older submission copies can be deleted on request. [Formspree security](https://formspree.io/security/) |
| Google email | Confirm mailbox plan and applicable data terms. Google's published Workspace DPA alone does not establish coverage for a consumer Gmail account. [Google Workspace DPA](https://workspace.google.com/terms/dpa_terms.html) |

Record unresolved items in the [Compliance log](compliance-log.md). Keep vendor credentials and DPA copies private.
