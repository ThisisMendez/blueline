# Blueline Lease

**Official product name:** Blueline Lease. **Domain:** `bluelinelease.com`. **Project shorthand:** Blueline.

**Campaign motto:** “Blueline Redline. Draw the line before you sign.” Use it as a callout beside the homepage headline. Use Blueline Lease in the wordmark, browser titles, and product copy.

Blueline Lease helps renters, and landlords too, see what their residential agreement could mean for their side before they sign. The user picks the side; the report highlights the clauses that could harm it. Present the product as a self-check. The user decides whether and how to act.

The brand is calm, precise, and quietly defiant: show the source, explain the consequence, and make room for a better reply. Agreements should feel like working documents, not intimidating legal artifacts.

## Visual direction

**Blueprint + Signal Red** is the selected palette. It should feel editorial and decisive.

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blueprint navy | `#123B5D` | Structure, navigation, and trust. |
| Paper | `#F6F1E7` | Readable document surfaces and a human, editorial feel. |
| Signal red | `#E5453A` | Consequential terms and credible proposed changes; use sparingly. Use Red Ink (`#C23327`) for the underlined “Redline” in the homepage motto. |

Blueprint blue roots the name in documents and structure. Signal red makes “Redline” legible at a glance without making the whole interface feel anxious. The visual system should help a signer move from an exact source sentence to its consequence and then to a proposed change.

## The landing page

The landing page is the one Persuade-mode surface in this product; everything past sign-in is Operate mode. It earns attention on a real, specific mechanism, not a mood.

- **Lead with the role-tailored self-check.** The homepage opens with “Understand your rental lease.” and “Spot risky terms and see the exact clause behind each flag.” Show how the report gives the selected party a practical question or proposed change. Any worked report example shows one role.
- **Proof is a worked example, not a claim about people.** The evidence base has no confirmed customer, testimonial, or usage statistic (see PRODUCT.md's Evidence on Hand). Until real ones exist, "proof" on this page means showing the mechanism working — an example cited flag, its source sentence, and its counter-offer — not a quote, a number, or a logo.
- **Keep red for meaningful marks.** Use it for consequential terms and the underlined “Redline” in the motto. Use blueprint navy for the primary action.
- **Use one primary action.** “Review a lease” opens the review page. State plainly that the product is free to use for this version.
