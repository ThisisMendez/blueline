# Blueline Redline

**Official product name:** Blueline Redline. **Project shorthand:** Blueline.

Blueline Redline helps people see what an agreement is really asking of them before they commit. It starts with renters facing a lease, but the brand can extend to other moments where fine print can change a decision. The first product version remains focused on lease signers.

The brand is calm, precise, and quietly defiant: show the source, explain the consequence, and make room for a better reply. Agreements should feel like working documents, not intimidating legal artifacts.

## Visual direction

**Blueprint + Signal Red** is the selected palette. It should feel editorial and decisive.

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blueprint navy | `#123B5D` | Structure, navigation, and trust. |
| Paper | `#F6F1E7` | Readable document surfaces and a human, editorial feel. |
| Signal red | `#E5453A` | Consequential terms and credible proposed changes; use sparingly. |

Blueprint blue roots the name in documents and structure. Signal red makes “Redline” legible at a glance without making the whole interface feel anxious. The visual system should help a signer move from an exact source sentence to its consequence and then to a proposed change.
