# 42. Learn what a Dominican Republic pilot would require

Status: ready-for-agent
Stage: Later · Discovery eligible to run alongside Rhode Island
Target: v0.6.0 candidate for a limited pilot, conditional on discovery, review and separate launch approval
Decision: approved for planning November 18, 2026. Begin discovery only when authorized. This does not authorize launch, live uploads, paid work or use of real lease data.

Use public official sources and synthetic Dominican Spanish examples to assess a possible long-term residential pilot. Keep Rhode Island as the first market.

## Discovery deliverable

- Document the audience and questions about demand, the file and language formats people receive, and what the present review experience would need to handle in Dominican Spanish.
- Identify official nationwide rental references and their ongoing maintenance needs. List open questions for local review about personal information, cross-border transfers and deletion, product language, and local consumer terms.
- Outline a distinct quality evaluation using synthetic examples first, exact Spanish quotes, and local terminology. Identify what evidence would be required before opening a pilot.
- Bring back a recommendation and blockers for a separate go/no-go decision. Do not enable the market or make a commitment to launch.
